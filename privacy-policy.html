Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Ace Gaming receives through the website and WhatsApp booking flow, why it is used and the choices available to customers.

Effective date: 28 July 2026
Privacy Policy → Terms of Service →

A clear overview of how Ace Gaming handles website enquiries and booking information.

1. Information we collect

When you contact Ace Gaming or submit a booking request, we may receive information such as your name, phone number, selected service, number of players, preferred date and time, and any notes you include. Our website host may also process basic technical information needed to deliver and secure the website.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to questions and process booking requests.
  • To confirm session details, availability and changes.
  • To provide customer support and maintain venue safety.
  • To improve our website, packages and services.
  • To comply with lawful requests and protect our rights.

3. WhatsApp and external services

The booking form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. The customer chooses whether to send that message. Information sent through WhatsApp is also handled under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy practices. Other external links, such as social media or maps, may operate under their own policies.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with staff or service providers who need it to manage bookings, operate the website, support customers or meet legal obligations.

5. Data retention

Booking and enquiry information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, including customer support, accounting, dispute handling, security and legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of record.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No website, messaging platform or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information that we control, or to withdraw a request where applicable. We may need to verify the request before acting on it and may retain information where required for lawful purposes.

8. Minors

Where parental or guardian approval is required, a booking for a minor should be made or approved by a parent or guardian. Customers should provide only the information needed to arrange the session.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or practices change. The effective date at the top of the page shows the latest published version.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to Ace Gaming on WhatsApp at +91 90760 60602.
